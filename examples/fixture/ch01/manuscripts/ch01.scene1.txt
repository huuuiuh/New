The measurement device screamed.

[F]

Red letters. The same red letters as ten years ago.

“F-rank. Porter registration is the window on your left.”

The officer was already calling the next name. Jung Min-jae, according to the badge. He did not look up. He never had.

Do-yoon looked down at his palm. It wasn’t shaking. There was no reason for it to shake. In this whole room, he was the only one who knew how far this hand could go.

*March twelfth.*

Gangnam. Three things to finish before then.

Behind him someone laughed, the short bark of a man who had just read a B on the panel and wanted the room to know it. Ten years ago Do-yoon had turned around to look. This time he didn’t. He knew the man’s name, knew the guild that would sign him, knew the gate that would swallow him in year four.

None of that was his problem today.

“Next. Kang Do-yoon, step away from the device.”

“Yes, sir.”

He stepped away. The panel went dark behind him, and a nineteen-year-old with dyed hair took his place and held her breath the way everyone did.

Do-yoon walked toward the window on the left. His legs remembered the route better than his head did; he had walked it once before, on the worst morning of a life that had ended badly.

The morning was the same. The building was the same. The letter on the panel was the same.

He was not.
